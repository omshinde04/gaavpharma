3. Step-by-Step Live Chemist Demonstration Script (15-Minute Test)

Follow this exact interactive script when sitting with the medical store owner to demonstrate GaavPharma's next-level capabilities:

1 Instant PIN Keypad Login & Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Action: On the lockscreen keypad, enter PIN 1111 to log in as Store Owner (Divya Kale).

  • What to highlight: No cumbersome usernames or passwords to remember. Fast 4-digit PIN designed for quick cashier shift changes.
  • Security Feature: Cashiers can only bill and hold bills. Wholesale purchase prices, distributor debt, and settings are securely locked to the Store Admin.
2 High-Speed Counter Billing & Mouse-Free Keyboard Operation

Action: In the search box, type Do or Aug. Press ↓ (Arrow Down) to navigate search results, then press Enter.

  • What to highlight: The medicine is instantly added to the bill with FIFO batch selection, expiry date, rack location, and unit rate without ever touching the mouse!
  • Generic Salt Substitutes: If a medicine is out of stock, click "Find Substitutes" to view all in-stock medicines sharing the exact generic chemical composition (e.g. Paracetamol 650mg).
  • Fast Payment Toggle: Press F8 to instantly cycle between Cash, UPI (auto-displays dynamic QR code), Khata (Customer Credit), and Card.
3 Peak-Hour Rush: "Hold & Resume" Counter Bills (F6)

Action: While 2 medicines are in the cart, press F6. The counter clears instantly for the next customer. Then press F6 again and click "Resume".

  • What to highlight: In busy evening rush hours, customers often forget their wallet or ask for an additional medicine from a nearby clinic. In MediVision, the bill must be cancelled. In GaavPharma, F6 parks the bill in a queue so you can bill 5 other customers and resume in 1 second.
4 Statutory FDA Schedule H1 Compliance Register (Rule 65)

Action: Add a Schedule H1 medicine (e.g., Augmentin 625 Duo). Notice the red Schedule H1 badge. Enter Patient Name, Village/Address, Prescribing Doctor, and Doctor's MMC Reg No. Press F9 to complete sale.

  • What to highlight: Navigate to the Compliance & GST Hub tab. Show the medical owner the digital Schedule H1 Register. Every prescription sale is legally logged with serial number, batch, patient name, and doctor registration number ready for Drug Inspector inspection.
5 Wholesale Purchase Inwarding & Distributor Ledger ("Accounts Payable")

Action: Click Purchases & Distributors (F7). Click "+ Inward Wholesale Bill". Select distributor (e.g. Mahaveer Pharma), enter invoice number, medicine batch, purchase rate, and free scheme (e.g. 10 + 2 Free).

  • What to highlight: Free scheme items are automatically added to inventory stock (12 units total). Show the distributor ledger: each inward bill automatically adds to the pending balance, and payments made reduce the balance with reference numbers.
6 Section 34 CGST Expiry Debit Notes & Wholesaler Returns

Action: In Purchases, click "+ Issue GST Debit Note". Select expired medicine batch and claim quantity.

  • What to highlight: Automatically generates an official GST Debit Note (`DN-XXXXXX`). Inventory stock is immediately deducted, the distributor's accounts payable balance is reduced by the claim amount, and the GSTR-3B tax summary reverses the Input Tax Credit (ITC) legally under Section 34.
7 Auto-Shortage Diary & 1-Click WhatsApp Purchase Order

Action: Open the Shortage Diary tab. Show items automatically added due to low stock. Click the green WhatsApp button next to any distributor.

  • What to highlight: A beautifully formatted purchase order is generated instantly containing your store name, drug license numbers (20B/21B), item names, strip sizes, and suggested order quantities—ready to send to the distributor via WhatsApp with 1 click!
8 Daily Shop Exit: Dual PC + USB Pendrive Safety Backup Engine

Action: Click the red Logout button in the top right header (or press Shift + L).

  • The Visual Wow Factor: The modal "Take Daily Safety Backup Before Exit" appears displaying both Local PC Hard Drive (`D:\GaavPharma_Backups`) and Removable USB Pendrive.
  • Click [ 1-Click Safe Backup & Exit ]. Watch the 3-step progress bar (WAL Flush ➔ Dual File Copy ➔ 30-Day Auto Pruning) finish in 1.5 seconds and return to the secure lockscreen.
  • Disaster Recovery Assurance: Explain to the owner: "Even if your computer crashes tomorrow or is stolen, your entire store's inventory, khata credit, and bills are safe on your pendrive. Plug it into any laptop, click Restore, and you are back in business in 2 seconds!"
